# Privacy Policy

**App:** SoilMate
**Last Updated:** 2 April 2026

## 1. Introduction

SoilMate helps users manage their plant collection, track care tasks, and receive reminders. This document explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, and how it is stored.

By using SoilMate, you agree to the practices described below.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you register an account, we collect and store in our database:
- Email address
- Display name
- Password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
- Location (predefined representative regional cities), timezone, notificationreminder and calendar sync preferences

### 2.2 Plant and Care Data
The following are stored in our database to provide the core functionality of the app:
- Plants you add to your collection (nickname, location label, notes)
- Care tasks (watering, feeding schedules)
- Care activity logs (when you watered, fed, or skipped a task)
- Barcodes scanned to identify plants

### 2.3 Location and Date/Time
- We collect your **approximate location** to adjust care schedules based on local season and climate.
- We collect **date and time** information together with care activities to generate accurate reminders and schedules.

### 2.4 Photos
- The app accesses your **camera** to scan plant barcodes (QR codes) and to take photos of your plants and care logs.
- **Photos are stored locally on your device only.** They are **not** uploaded to our database or any server.

### 2.5 Calendar Access
- With your permission, the app accesses your device **Calendar** to **create, edit, and remove events** related to plant care reminders.
- We do not read, collect, or store any other events from your calendar.

### 2.6 Push Notifications
- The app uses Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to send care reminders.
- A device token is stored in our database so the server can deliver notifications to your device.
- You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings or within the app.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected only to:
- Run the core features of the app (plant tracking, scheduling, reminders)
- Personalise care schedules based on your location and timezone
- Send push notifications you have opted into
- Sync care reminders to your calendar

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- Account, plant, care, and device-token data are stored in a **MySQL database** on the project's backend server.
- All communication between the app and the server uses **HTTPS**.
- Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.
- Photos remain on your device and are never transmitted to the server.
